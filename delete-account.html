Delete your Marginalia account

You can delete your account and all its data at any time.

In the app

  • Open Marginalia and go to your Profile.
  • Tap the "..." menu and then Settings.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm by typing your username.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, write to soporte@catonmars.com from the email you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account" and your username. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, photo, shelf, ratings, reviews, quotes, lists, comments, likes, followers and following, progress and reading days.
  • Your reports and blocks.

Books (titles and covers) aren't your data and stay in the catalog. We don't keep copies of your data after deleting it.
